One semantic tree per formula, two renderers. The right pane is laid out by the FA-RTL rules of the framework (R1–R5, D1–D12); the left pane by the INTL renderer, from the same tree.

Nothing on the right is typed as a string. Digit order, mirroring, stretching and script placement are decisions of the renderer; the tree stores values, roles and styles only (L0). Open “tree” under any formula to see the interchange form.

What is real here

  • Every layout rule of L1, L2, L3 and L5 that the ten formulas touch, in a ~450-line engine; the INTL pane is the bijection made visible.
  • The fonts ship with the page (Noto Naskh Arabic, Vazirmatn, Noto Sans Math; SIL Open Font License). Looped, double-struck and tailed letters are the actual U+1EE00 code points; the stretch is U+0640 kashida inside a joined token.
  • Numbers are stored once, canonically (1234.56); the Persian pane never stores a reversed string.

What is simulated or missing

  • Ordinary variables are drawn with plain naskh letters; plain-text export would use the isolated forms of the U+1EE00 block.
  • The marked face is a sans digit design in colour, standing in for a dedicated mathematical digit style (D4); the U1 characters do not exist yet, so plain-text export still needs the interim encoding of L2.
  • پ (π) has no looped code point: it renders through the declared fallback of D6, a naskh letter with a loop mark.
  • No editing yet; that is Phase 3. Spacing and sizes are first approximations, not a finished math typesetting standard.
